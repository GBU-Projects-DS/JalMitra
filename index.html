Learn the basics

Too much or too little?

Yellow, soft leaves and soggy soil often mean too much water. Dry, crispy edges and wilting in dry soil mean too little. Check soil, not just the leaves.

Best time to water

Early morning. Less water is lost to heat and wind, and leaves dry out during the day.

Use mulch

Straw, dry leaves or crop residue on top of the soil slows evaporation and keeps moisture steady.

Harvest rainwater

Store monsoon runoff in tanks or ponds and feed it to your drip system later in the dry months.

Check the soil first

Push a finger 2 to 3 cm into the soil. If it feels dry, water. If it feels damp, wait.

Water deeply, not often

Deep, less frequent watering pushes roots down so plants handle dry spells better.

Why it matters in India

Falling groundwater

Many regions depend on borewells and groundwater is dropping in several states. Pumping only when needed saves water and electricity.

Uneven monsoon

Rain is late or patchy in many years. A moisture-based system waters when soil is dry, not by the calendar.

Support for drip

Central and state schemes such as Per Drop More Crop under PMKSY have supported micro-irrigation. Eligibility and subsidy change, so check with your state agriculture department.

Works anywhere

Works on any phone or computer with nothing to install, so farmers, students and gardeners everywhere can use it.

Schemes and support in India

PMKSY: Per Drop More Crop

Central scheme that supports drip and sprinkler micro-irrigation for farmers.

PM-KUSUM

Supports solar pumps and solarisation of farm pumps, useful for off-grid smart irrigation.

State and horticulture missions

Many states add their own micro-irrigation support, often through horticulture or agriculture departments.

Scheme names, rules and subsidy rates change and differ by state. Confirm on official portals or at your district agriculture office before applying.

FAQ

How accurate is the calculator?

It gives estimates from typical crop water needs. Real needs depend on variety, weather and soil, so check the soil with your finger first.

Where is my data saved?

My Plants are saved only in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data will remove them.

Can I use it for a farm?

Yes, choose area in acres. For large farm decisions, check with a local agriculture expert.

Is this professional advice?

No, it is information and estimates only.
